# Robotic Arm Mounting Infrastructure

Mechanical mounting systems developed for the Team Vyadh robotic arm.

These mounting systems were designed for:

* vibration resistance
* modularity
* compact integration
* competition deployment reliability

---

## MD10C Motor Driver Mount

Custom mounting enclosure developed for Cytron MD10C motor drivers used in the competition robotic arm.

The mount was:

* 3D printed
* integrated into the final competition arm
* designed for stable driver retention during robotic arm operation

---

## PCB Retention Mount

Custom PCB mounting system designed to securely hold a custom PCB/protoboard assembly inside the robotic arm.

The mount used controlled sliding tolerances to:

* prevent PCB movement during arm motion
* improve vibration resistance
* simplify installation and servicing

---

## Wire Management System

Custom wire routing and cable management system developed for robotic arm wiring organization.

The objective was to:

* reduce cable interference
* improve routing stability
* prevent wire entanglement during arm articulation
